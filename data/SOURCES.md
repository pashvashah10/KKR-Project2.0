## Notes on figure selection
- **Profit line used per club is labelled** in the data (`reported_as`): most clubs report a pre-tax figure; Brighton's headline is an operating profit before interest & tax. The model treats them per their label and never silently equates them.
- **Wages/revenue** and all margins are **derived by the model** from the sourced primitives, so a ratio can never disagree with its inputs. Where a source additionally quotes a ratio, it is noted but not stored.
- Same-season panel: all six seed clubs are **2023/24** for valid cross-sectional comparison. 2024/25 figures (e.g. Man Utd Sky link) are logged for the backtest/timeseries layer, not the cross-section.
