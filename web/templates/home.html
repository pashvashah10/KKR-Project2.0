The decision that makes this different

Local temperature is regressed on global temperature, not on the year.

Fitting a trend against calendar time forces a shape onto the past that the record does not have — the 1940–75 aerosol plateau, the volcanic notches after Agung, El Chichón and Pinatubo, the post-1980 steepening. A straight line averages all of it, then extrapolates the average for another century.

Regressing on modelled global temperature reproduces those features without being told they exist, and the coefficient is the regional amplification factor: degrees local per degree global. Extrapolating then needs no assumption about the shape of the future, only a choice of scenario.

Three more choices in the same spirit: standard errors from a year-block bootstrap, because daily weather is not 36,525 independent observations; extreme value theory in the tails, because that is where a weather product pays; and hedge sizing against ruin probability rather than expected value, because every hedge loses money in expected dollars.

Where we are honest

  • Out-of-sample skill against climatology is in the low single digits. For daily temperature at 30-year lead, climatology is a very strong baseline. The value is in the trend, the tails and the distribution.
  • Amplification is weakly identified at some sites, because forcing and calendar time are near-collinear over a century. Where the estimate is specification-sensitive, the diagnostics say so.
  • Raw station records carry undocumented moves and instrument changes. We detect and flag them. We do not correct them.
  • We are not an insurer, a broker or an MGA. Parametric pricing here is indicative and subject to underwriting.

Basis risk, priced explicitly

A contract settles on a gauge, not on your car park. That distance costs money, and so does the vertical gap — a gauge 9 km away but 800 m lower is separated by roughly 5 °C of lapse rate and sits on the wrong side of the rain/snow line for much of the season.

Both go into the match and both appear as their own line in the quote. Most of the industry buries this inside a margin.
