Or drive it from your own systems

Everything the page above does is a public endpoint. Booking systems, BI tools and finance stacks can call it directly.

# sign up once; the key is shown once
curl -X POST $API/v1/accounts -d '{"name":"...","email":"..."}'

# add a venue --- returns 202 with a job to poll
curl -X POST $API/v1/sites -H "authorization: Bearer $KEY" \
  -d '{"name":"...","lat":32.7796,"lon":-79.9245,"vertical":"Waterfront venue",
       "season_start_month":3,"season_end_month":11,"variable_cost_ratio":0.32}'

# your daily revenue --- the single most valuable input
curl -X POST $API/v1/sites/$SITE/revenue -F file=@revenue.csv

# your exposure, suggested terms, a price, and how much to buy
curl $API/v1/sites/$SITE/exposure
curl $API/v1/sites/$SITE/suggested-terms
curl -X POST $API/v1/sites/$SITE/quote  -d '{...}'
curl -X POST $API/v1/sites/$SITE/hedge  -d '{...}'

Full reference at /docs.

What to be honest about. Without your revenue the loss curve is fitted against placeholder data and describes a plausible business rather than yours. Out-of-sample skill against plain climatology is a couple of percent — the value is in the trend, the tails and the distribution, not in daily point forecasts. Sites whose station record has an unexplained discontinuity are flagged and not quoted.
