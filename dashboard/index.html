The franchise-of-franchises

Arctos built a firm on one insight: elite franchises are a scarce, appreciating, largely uncorrelated asset class, and a diversified basket of stakes across leagues compounds like little else in private markets. This screen maps the operators already living that thesis — single brands and shared infrastructure spread across leagues, sports, geographies and, above all, the calendar.

Why this is the KKR view. KKR is an Arctos backer, and Arctos's edge is buying into networks, not single teams — a minority stake in an operator like the Knight Riders or Mumbai Indians buys year-round, cross-geography content and a repeatable playbook, not one club's results. The cricket networks are early in that build; City Football Group proved it in football; Boehly's group shows the cross-sport version. This screen is the map of where a franchise-of-franchises stake creates the most diversified, calendar-spanning exposure — the native habitat of the thesis, in the APAC market that sits closest to the desk.
